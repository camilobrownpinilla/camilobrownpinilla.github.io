I am a senior undergraduate at Harvard University studying Applied Mathematics. My research interests lie at the intersection of Geometric ML and Natural Language Processing. I am particularly interested characterizing the (approximate) symmetries of meaning, interpreting and manipulating learned representations and their geometries, and designing language representations and models that respect symmetry.
